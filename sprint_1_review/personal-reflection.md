# Sprint 1 — Personal Reflection

## Key Learning
What was the most important lesson you learned about how work actually flows during a
sprint?
- I learned that User stories on a sprint depend on each other and that if a User Story was not successfully completed it will block the upcoming sprint and reduce the team's productivity
- Planning and gathering resources early is effective because it helps with the success of a sprint
- The importance of a Daily standups because a lot can change during the sprint and those changes have to be communicated with the team
- Time is limited and it's important to prioritise core features

## Assumptions That Failed
Which assumption (planning, estimation, communication, execution) broke down the most?
- The assumptions that broke down the most were Estimation and Execution. The sprint goal was partially met because the team was experiencing technical issues from the start as we estimated for a simple environment setup but things failed. The team assumed all 4 User Stories could be completed in Sprint 1 but alot of blockers were identified in the daily standups which required scope adjustments. The team was also not able to execute all tasks within the assumed time frame which led to extending the period of Sprint 1

## Behavioural Change
What is one concrete behaviour you would change in Sprint 2?
- One concrete behaviour I would change is estimating the Sprint tasks and outcomes realistically, and also waiting for the next daily standup to report blockers instead of doing more research on how to solve the issue

## Professional Growth
What skill or habit do you need to strengthen to perform better in a real Scrum team?
- Communicating with my team and tracking progress to ensure that we're all on the same track and no one is being left behind
- Estimating tasks and goals realistically because when things fail I will have to make adjustments to my planning
- Also the tasks prioritisation and breakdown so that I can be able to plan which tasks go into which sprint to better accelerate end of sprint delivery.
